AspirantPro - AI MCQ Generator for Students

AspirantPro helps students convert PDF books, PDF notes, photo notes and study material into fresh exam-style MCQ mock tests. Students can practice one question at a time, submit the test, check score, view correct answers and read explanations.

Key features

  • Generate MCQs from PDF, TXT, DOCX and photo notes.
  • Chapter-wise dropdown for text PDF books when chapters are detected.
  • Book title detection and all-page compressed full-book context for better MCQs.
  • Global student review system with scrollable review list.
  • Fresh random mock test every attempt from the same study material.
  • One-by-one real exam style question screen.
  • Answers and explanations only after submitting the mock test.

Privacy Policy

AspirantPro may use Google login for student profile. Uploaded files are processed in the browser and AI request is sent for MCQ generation. Students should upload only study material they have permission to use.
